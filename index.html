About Me

I am Zhehan (Michael) Zhang (张哲晗), an undergraduate in Applied Physics at Guohao College, Tongji University, through the Strong Foundation Program. I expect to graduate in June 2027.

My research interests center on LLM agents and multi-agent systems, particularly memory and reliable execution in long-horizon tasks. I am also interested in human–AI collaboration and multimodal learning.

I am currently an AI Agent Engineering Intern at REDnote (Xiaohongshu), Lab1327, working on Loomind. My research projects span agent-assisted visualization, visualization framing, multimodal emotion recognition, and medical imaging communication.
